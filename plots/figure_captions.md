# Figure captions

## Figure 1

Study design and record selection. Three phenotype-based rules and a gene-based comparator were applied to the same ClinVar export. Each branch reports its number of distinct VariationIDs; annotated gene memberships are summarized separately. The curated-gene comparator includes records across annotated conditions.

## Figure 2

Record-set size and classification composition across selection rules. (A) Distinct VariationIDs selected by the phrase, expanded-text and HPO-linked rules and the gene comparator. (B) Classification proportions among all selected records in each set, regardless of review status. P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, variant of uncertain significance; B/LB, benign/likely benign.

## Figure 3

Review-status distribution under the phrase rule. (A) Classification categories by official review stars for 18,384 VariationIDs. (B) Composition of the 173 expert-panel records. The Other category represents one drug-response record (n = 1). No VariationIDs had 4-star review status (practice guidelines; n = 0). P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, variant of uncertain significance; B/LB, benign/likely benign.

## Figure 4

Gene-level classification profiles under the phrase rule. Points represent 123 genes with at least ten distinct VariationIDs. Orange highlights DIAPH1 and MET; blue highlights MYO7A and SLC26A4; grey marks the other 119 genes. (A) Counts of P/LP records with at least two review stars versus VUS-plus-conflicting counts; values from 0 to 1 are shown linearly and values above 1 logarithmically on symmetric-log axes. (B) Corresponding within-gene proportions. Each VariationID contributes once to each annotated gene, so gene counts are nonadditive. P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, variant of uncertain significance.

## Figure 5

Gene rankings by conflict count and proportion. Genes had at least ten annotated VariationIDs. (A) Twelve largest conflict counts. (B) Twelve largest conflict proportions, with gene-level denominators shown as n. Panels are ranked independently; tied ranks are ordered alphabetically by gene symbol. Single-gene sensitivity results are in S5 Table.
