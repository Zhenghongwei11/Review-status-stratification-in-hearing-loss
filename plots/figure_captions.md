# Figure captions

## Figure 1

Study design and record selection. The three phenotype-based sets are nested: hearing-loss phrase, expanded text, and expanded text plus HPO-linked diseases. A separate comparator retrieves records across annotated conditions for 164 genes with qualifying ClinGen gene–disease evidence. Each set is summarized separately by VariationID. Comparisons address record coverage and disease context, classification and review-status composition, and gene-level counts and proportions. Main-text gene profiles use the phrase-selected set.

## Figure 2

Record-set size and classification composition across selection rules. (A) Distinct VariationIDs selected by the phrase, expanded-text and HPO-linked rules and the gene comparator. (B) Classification proportions among all selected records in each set, regardless of review status. P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, variant of uncertain significance; B/LB, benign/likely benign.

## Figure 3

Review-status distribution under the phrase rule. (A) Classification categories by official review stars for 18,384 VariationIDs. (B) Composition of the 173 expert-panel records. In panel B, Other represents one drug-response record (n = 1). No VariationIDs had 4-star review status (practice guidelines; n = 0). P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, variant of uncertain significance; B/LB, benign/likely benign.

## Figure 4

Gene-level classification profiles under the phrase rule. Points represent 123 genes with at least ten distinct VariationIDs. Orange highlights DIAPH1 and MET, which lead by VUS-plus-conflicting count and proportion, respectively. Blue highlights MYO7A and SLC26A4, the two genes contributing the most P/LP records with at least two review stars; grey marks the other 119 genes. (A) Counts of P/LP records with at least two review stars versus VUS-plus-conflicting counts; values from 0 to 1 are shown linearly and values above 1 logarithmically on symmetric-log axes. (B) Corresponding within-gene proportions. Each VariationID contributes once to each annotated gene, so gene counts are nonadditive. P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, variant of uncertain significance.

## Figure 5

Gene rankings by conflict count and proportion. Genes had at least ten annotated VariationIDs. (A) Twelve largest conflict counts. (B) Twelve largest conflict proportions, with gene-level denominators shown as n. Proportions based on few records are more sensitive to changes in individual classifications. Panels are ranked independently; tied ranks are ordered alphabetically by gene symbol. Single-gene sensitivity results are in S5 Table.
